The open book

Clicking a spine turns it face-on in its slot, as it does today. The small card gains a Show more button, and that opens a full-screen spread: the book's name and picture on the left page, its description and a PDF download on the right.

Five directions below, all drawn from the live stylesheet's tokens. Every one takes a `cover` image path and a `pdf` path in curriculum.json; with no cover the left page draws a kitab cover from the book's own leather and cartouche colours, and with no pdf the download link is simply absent, the way fees.cta stays hidden while null. The right page scrolls, so descriptions are no longer held to the small card's two sentences.

Quiet spread

The restrained one: a single wide sheet, a hairline gutter with a breath of shadow, deep margins and nothing else. No leather, no jadwal, no board — the cover and the big Cormorant name carry it, the download is a gold underlined link rather than a button, and the type has the most room of any option here.

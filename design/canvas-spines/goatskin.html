Curriculum · the spines · round 2

Tooled goatskin

Round 1's first binding (the braided border, the pendant and the ogee cartouche) in four new arrangements of dark green and gold. Round 1

1 · Gold cartouche 2 · Gilt ground 3 · Emerald panel 4 · Gold compartments
1

Gold cartouche

Green goatskin with the gold braid and pendant, but the cartouche is solid gold and the title is lettered in dark green, like a gold title-piece set into the leather. It is the brightest on the shelf, and the title reads at about 8:1. Hover a book on the shelf to lean it out.

2

Gilt ground

The reverse arrangement: the spine is antique gold, tooled in dark green. The braid, frame, pendant and lozenge are green, and the green cartouche carries the title in gold with a gold line inside its edge. The subject is dark green on the gold. It stands out most against the dark wells.

3

Emerald panel

Two greens. The edges are near-black green carrying the gold braid, the space inside them is a lighter emerald panel framed in gold, and the cartouche is near-black again. Of the four it stays closest to round 1, with more depth. The title is pale gold on the dark cartouche, about 12:1.

4

Gold compartments

Green goatskin with solid gold compartments at the head and foot, each inside double gilt fillets. The head has the pendant set into it in green, and the foot carries the subject in dark green on gold. Between them the braid and the dark cartouche with its gold title, about 12:1.

Curriculum · the spines · round 3

Point, then open

Point at a book and its small card appears beside it; click the book and the full page (the folio) opens in its place. Four styles for the card, all on the emerald-panel spines. Round 2

1 · Cream leaf 2 · Emerald plate 3 · Pulled slip 4 · Swung board
1

Cream leaf

Today's card, cream in a gilt rule, lifted off the shelf. It floats beside the book, resting on the shelf line, with a notch pointing at the spine, and slides in as the book leans out. Near the right edge of the screen it moves to the book's left. Try it: point at a book, then click it. Escape closes.

2

Emerald plate

A card bound like the book: a near-black green edge with the gold braid down both sides, an emerald panel, a gold title and cream text. It is centred on the book's height and tied to it by a short gold line. Try it: point at a book, then click it. Escape closes.

3

Pulled slip

As the book leans out, a manuscript leaf slides out from behind it, like a note kept between the pages. The leaf has the same ruled border (ink, red and gold) and paper as the folio, so the preview already looks like the page it opens to, and a red ribbon marks it. Try it: point at a book, then click it. Escape closes.

4

Swung board

The book stays upright and its front board swings open on the hinge beside the spine, in 3D. The text is printed on the cream endpaper inside, and the board has the book's own green edge and gold line. The most playful of the four, and the card is exactly as tall as the book. Try it: point at a book, then click it. Escape closes.

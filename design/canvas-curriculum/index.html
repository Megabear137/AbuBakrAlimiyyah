Beyond the shelf

Six ways to show the seven years' books, each readable at a glance and opened with a click. Built on the live stylesheet, reading content/curriculum.json.

1

Cover carousel your idea

A tab per year over a carousel of the year's covers. Clicking a cover lifts it and opens a board beneath it, its notch pointing at the cover, with the subject, title, description and See more. Try it: switch years, click a cover, click another.

2

The pile your idea

A tab per year. The year's books lie in a pile on the left, titles readable along their spines. Clicking a spine draws that book out of the pile: its cover stands beside the pile and its description beside the cover, and the book shown before goes back on the pile. Try it: click the spines.

3

The path

The seven years as stations on one line, so the length and order of the programme shows at a glance and the line fills up to the chosen year. The year's books are cream cards (cover, subject, title, first line). A card opens the full view in a dialog, like the folio. Try it: pick a year, open a card.

4

Syllabus grid

The whole programme at once: subjects down the side, years across the top, a title in each cell, edged in its binding colour. It shows how a subject runs from year to year. Clicking a title opens its details under the grid. On phones the grid scrolls sideways with the subjects pinned. Try it: click any title.

5

Contents page

A kitab's fihrist. Each year is one row with its numeral, its subjects and a fan of its covers. The row opens into a numbered list of books with dotted leaders to the subject, and a book opens into its description. The quietest option, and it reads well on a phone. Try it: open a year, then a book.

6

Turn the card

A rail of the years (a row of them on phones) beside a grid of covers, each captioned with its title and subject. Clicking a cover turns it over to the board side, which holds the subject, title, description and See more. Clicking again turns it back. Try it: click a cover.

I

Bento

A segmented year switch whose gold thumb slides to the chosen year, over a bento grid: one book large on the board with its cover and description, the other five as cream tiles numbered 01–06. Clicking a tile moves that book into the large cell, and the tiles glide to their new places. Try it: click a tile, then switch years.

II

Year lanes

All seven years at once as tall board panels, each with its numeral and name running up it, so the length of the programme is clear from the start. Clicking a lane widens it to show that year's books in two columns, and a book opens its description at the lane's foot. On phones the lanes stack as rows that open downward. Try it: open another year.

III

Library

Every book in one list, grouped under sticky year headings, with subject chips (each with its colour dot) and a year switch to narrow it. The chosen book sits in a detail pane beside the list. It answers "what Arabic is taught, and when?" in one click. Try it: pick Arabic, then All years.

IV

Roadmap

Scroll through the years. A large numeral and a seven-step progress track stay pinned at the side and follow along (pinned along the top on phones); clicking a step jumps to that year. Each book is a compact card, and it opens in a drawer that slides in from the right, with Previous and Next to move through the year. Try it: scroll, then open a book.

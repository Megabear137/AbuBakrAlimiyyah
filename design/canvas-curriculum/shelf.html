R2

Roadmap · wall shelf

The same scroll, with each year's books on a small wall shelf only as wide as the books, hung on two wooden brackets, so it reads as seven shelves up a wall. The year's heading is a quiet centred line, since the pinned numeral already names it. The shelf widens when a book opens and takes the full column for the folio. Try it: open a book, then See more.

Tabs · plank

One year at a time. A sliding year switch names the year and its subjects, and below it that year's books stand on an oak plank cut to their width. Switching years brings the new books up onto the plank one after another. Try it: switch years, open a book.

T2

Tabs · label plates

The tabs are part of the shelf. The books stand on a long oak board whose front edge carries seven pinned brass label plates, like a library's shelf labels. The chosen plate is lit, and clicking another brings its year's books onto the board. Try it: click a plate.

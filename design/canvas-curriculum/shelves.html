S1

Floating slab

A thick oak slab on hidden fixings, so nothing shows beneath it but its shadow on the wall. The year is an engraved brass plate screwed to the slab's front at its four corners. Try it: point at a book, then click it.

S2

Carved corbels

A moulded board, with a lip along its top and a bead at its foot, carried on two scrolled corbels. The year is a gold cartouche with pointed ends, pinned to the board.

Curriculum

S3

Brass rods

A slim board with a brass strip along its edge, hung from two brass rods that rise past the books to roses on the wall. The year is a cream card in a brass holder clipped under the board, like a library's shelf label.

Curriculum

S4

Inlay and chains

A board let into the wall, two lines of gold stringing along its front and brass caps at its ends. The year hangs below it on two short chains, on a plaque cut from the dark board of the Details panels, lettered in gold.

E

Mamluk flap

The Cairo binding: goatskin in browns and oxblood, ruled by blind tooling (lines pressed in, not gilded), the title in a gilt almond. The lisan, the envelope flap that wraps an Islamic book's fore-edge, shows its pointed tongue at each spine's end. The cover has the almond, its two pendants, quarter-circle corner pieces and a blind-tooled frame. The most classical.

F

Ebru

Ottoman marbled paper in indigo, ochre, rust and cream, with leather at both ends and the title on a leather label ruled in gold. Each book shows a different part of the marbling. The cover is quarter-bound: a leather spine, leather corners, marbled boards and the same label. The most colourful.

G

Persian lacquer

Black and deep red lacquer with a gold vine running along both edges, budding red and green, and the title in italic inside a lobed gold cartouche. The cover frames the lacquer in the vine and sets the title in a large lobed cartouche. The richest and darkest.

Mihrab

Green, maroon and brown leather edged with a gold key border, with a small pointed arch at the spine's end. On the cover the title stands inside the arch, the same shape as the Alumni niches further down the page. Ties the shelf to the rest of the site.

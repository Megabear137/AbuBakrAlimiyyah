Jadwal, given room

Round 2's panel, unchanged in content. At full width, though, half a row was too narrow: the cover was cut down to a tall strip that read as stretched. Now the open bay widens across the row and its neighbour shrinks to a narrow bay beside it, so the cover gets a column wide enough for its own shape.

  • The open bay takes the row; the other bay narrows to 232px, keeps its books and its year label, and slides back when the book closes.
  • The cover column is 200–280px, so a 3:4 cover is as tall as the text beside it and still runs the full height of the left side.
  • Only where bays are paired — above 880px. Below that every bay already has the row to itself, and nothing changes.
  • Real pairing: Second Year shares its row with First Year and stands on the right, so the panel widens leftwards. Rounds 1 and 2 had it on the left, beside Third Year.

Still to settle

  • The cover is a new cover field in content/curriculum.json — "cover": "assets/img/books/achi-baatain.jpg". The placeholders here are drawn by generate_covers.py from the book's binding, which is also what a book without a photograph would fall back to.
  • Covers should be about 3:4. That is the shape the column is sized for. A cover of another shape still fills the column (object-fit: cover) and is trimmed rather than distorted, but photographs taken square-on, with a little margin round the boards, will fit best.
  • The cover never draws out past its shape. At full width it runs the whole left side. On a narrower screen (around 900–1100px) the text column is longer than a 3:4 cover is tall, so the cover stops at its own height, at the top of the leaf, and the text runs on beside the space under it.
  • The narrow bay stays usable. Clicking one of its books closes the panel first, so there is still only one book open across the shelf. On the site, with only two TODO slots in First Year, it fits without scrolling.
  • The author line is a placeholder in this mockup — author is empty for every book in curriculum.json today, and I have not invented any. When the field is empty the line is simply omitted.
  • Longer descriptions. The small card still clips at about two sentences; the panel holds four or five. Suggest keeping description short for the card and adding a long field the panel prefers when it is there.
  • The button question from round 1 still stands: the open card is itself a <button>, so “See more” cannot be a nested button. Either the card becomes a container with two real buttons (close / see more), or its click branches on the target as it does here.

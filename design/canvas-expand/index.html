Curriculum · the shelf

Opening a book in full

Clicking a spine still turns that book face-on in its slot, exactly as it does now. The small card gains a See more control; taking it expands the book to the whole bay — the cover on the left, the description on the right, in a manuscript hand. Closing returns the book to its small card, and the plank and year label never move.

Five directions below, each built on the live stylesheet and the real Second Year books. Every bay is shown paired, as it is on the site, so the panel is judged at the width it will actually get. The covers are placeholders drawn by generate_covers.py; the real ones will be image files named by a new cover field in curriculum.json.

1

Illuminated folio

One cream leaf filling the bay inside an illuminated border — a gold rule, a hairline within it and a rosette at each corner. The cover is propped in its own gilt frame, and the description opens on a gold drop cap. Nearest to the small card we already have, just given room; the quietest of the five.

Click a spine, then “See more”.
2

Opened codex

The panel is the book itself, lying open: two leaves with the fold shadowed down the middle, the cover propped on the left leaf with an italic caption, and the description on the right written over a scribe's ruling. The bay reads as a reading desk rather than a card.

Click a spine, then “Open the book”.
3

Illuminated unwan

The headpiece of a manuscript. A chevron band in the spines' own headband colours runs across the top of the panel, and the title sits in the spine's pointed cartouche laid over it, in gilt. The cover is set into a mihrab arch, the same shape as the testimony niches, and gold rosettes mark the meta line. The most ornamental, and the most tied to the rest of the page.

Click a spine, then “See more”.
4

Jadwal ruling

The scribe's own frame: a jadwal of thin–thick–thin rules in gold, ink and red round the whole leaf, an aged ground with faint foxing, the subject and author set out in a margin column as marginalia, and a red catchword at the foot where a manuscript names the next page's first word. The most literally a manuscript of the five, and the only one that leaves the site's cream for an aged one.

Click a spine, then “See more”.
5

Board & inset leaf

The panel stays wooden — cut from the same dark board as the Details cards, with a rust edge — so the shelf never turns into a sheet of paper. The cover lies straight on the board under a gold rule, the title is gilt on the wood, and only the description is a leaf of paper inset into it. The heaviest, and the one that sits most comfortably in a dark well.

Click a spine, then “See more”.

What each option assumes

  • A new cover field in content/curriculum.json, e.g. "cover": "assets/img/books/achi-baatain.jpg". A book without one falls back to the binding drawn from BINDINGS, the way the placeholder covers here are drawn, so the shelf keeps working while the masjid gathers photographs.
  • Longer descriptions become possible. The small card clips at about two sentences (.face__body { overflow: hidden }); the expanded panel holds four or five comfortably. The plan would be to keep description short for the card and add a long field the panel uses when it is there.
  • The panel replaces the well, not the bay's frame. The plank and the year label stay put, so the neighbouring bay in the row does not move — only this bay grows taller.
  • The open question is the button. Today the open card is the <button>. “See more” cannot be a nested button inside it, so when we build the chosen option the card should become a small container with two buttons (close, see more), or the card's click should branch on its target. The first is the honest one for screen readers; it costs the “the button is the card” rule in CLAUDE.md, which was written for a card with one action. Worth deciding before I write it.
  • Escape and focus. Escape would close the panel back to the small card (and a second Escape back to the spine), with focus returning to the spine each time. The bay's roving tabindex model is untouched while a panel is open.

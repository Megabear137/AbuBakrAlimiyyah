Five ways to make Our Teachers stand out

Today the section is three white cards on the white ground, between the green Details cards and the dark cork board, so it's the quietest part of the page. Each option below is built on the live stylesheet and reads content/teachers.json. Placeholder teachers (the TODO entries) are shown dimmed. A faded sliver of Details above and Articles below shows each option in place.

1 · Green hall 2 · Imam spotlight 3 · The chain 4 · Ijazah plates 5 · Names over the masjid
1

Green hall

The section becomes a full-bleed deep-green band with gilt rails and a faint star lattice, answering the full-bleed bookshelf. The teachers sit on it as cream cards with a double gold frame. The carousel and its arrows stay as they are. The smallest change: same carousel, new ground.

2

Imam spotlight

The first teacher in the list gets a large panel over the masjid photo, with a label taken from their bio. The rest form a numbered roster beside it, so all seven show at once and no carousel is needed. A featured: true flag in the JSON could choose who leads. Gives the page a face: the imam people already know.

3

The chain

The teachers are strung along a gilt cord as medallions, names alternating above and below, like an isnad. It picks up the intro's line, "text by text, with a teacher". Below 980px the cord turns upright into a list. The most distinctive of the five, and the most Islamic in feel.

4

Ijazah plates

Each teacher gets a cream plate modelled on an ijazah, with the spines' double gilt frame, the honorific in the kitab's pointed cartouche, the bio set in italic and a gold seal at the foot. The carousel sits in a mint panel so the section has its own colour. Ties the teachers to the bookshelf above.

5

Names over the masjid

A full-bleed band over the masjid photo, fixed so it shifts as you scroll, echoing the banner. The names are set large as a numbered list, and clicking one opens its bio. The heading and a count stay pinned on the left. The boldest: names treated like a title page. Try clicking one.

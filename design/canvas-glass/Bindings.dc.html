Anatomy of a spine

Achi BaatainUrdu
  1. Headband — striped silk peeking above the boards
  2. Head cap — the turned-over leather at the top
  3. Raised bands — the cords the pages are sewn on, each ruled in gilt
  4. Title label — a contrasting leather inlay with a gilt frame
  5. Subject — lettered in italic between the lower bands
  6. Fleurons — small gilt tools in the end panels
  7. Tail cap — finishes the foot

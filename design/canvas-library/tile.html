The bookstore's tile

Ten ways to dress the tile that stands in place of a book on the home page. Each is the whole section as the site draws it; only the tile changes.

1Unwan an illuminated headpiece over a ruled leaf, the heading in its cartouche

2Title page a leaf like the books', the heading as a kitab's title in a lobed frame, the link as the imprint

3Leather board a closed binding in oxblood, a gold-tooled frame and corner pieces, the heading gilt

4Stacked books the categories as books lying in a pile, a label on each fore-edge

5Fihrist the categories as a short index: rubric heading, Arabic-Indic counts on dotted leaders

6Colophon the tile set as the leaves' own colophon, large, tapering to the three dots

7Numeral the count in Arabic-Indic numerals in a verse-marker roundel

8Ebru marbled paper with a cream label mounted on it

9Brass plate the shelf's wood and a brass plate engraved with the heading

10Sheaf a leaf with two more fanned behind it; they spread when pointed at

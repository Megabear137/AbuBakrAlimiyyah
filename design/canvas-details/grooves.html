Details · round 2

Grooves, not gold

Round 1 chose option 1 for the titles — a Cormorant display title in bright gold. This round takes the gold out of every rule inside the two cards: the line under the title, the one between the part-time and full-time groups, the one under the fee tiers and the three between the discount rows. They are hard to read as gold hairlines on the dark board, and they compete with the figures, which are the only thing that should be gold.

Each option cuts them into the wood instead: a dark wall with a lit lower lip, so the line reads as depth rather than ink. Spacing is untouched — the gold borders are still there, just transparent, with the groove laid over them.

Incised hairline

One dark scratch, 2px deep: a black wall with a single lit pixel under it. The same weight as today's hairline, so nothing in the card moves, but it reads as a cut in the board rather than a line drawn on it. The quietest of the three, and the one that survives best on a phone.

Routed channel

A 6px channel with a shaded wall, a rounded bottom and a bright lip along the near edge — a groove run with a router bit, the same joinery as the shelf's wells. The deepest and most obviously carved; it also gives each card's sections real separation, at the cost of more shadow inside an already dark board.

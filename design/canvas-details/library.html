Details, from the library

Five ways to make the Details section part of the library: the fees, the family discounts and the class timings from program.json, word for word, with nothing added. Each mockup sits under the site's own section head and is built on the live stylesheet, so the grounds, the wood and the bays are the site's own. Use the switch in the corner for dark mode.

Two volumes, face-out

Part-time and Full-time are two books standing cover-out in one of the shelf's bays, bound like the curriculum (lapis and oxblood, gold tooling, corner pieces, a central mandorla with pendants). Each cover carries its fee and its hours, so the separate Class Timings heading goes. The discounts are a bookmark card leaning between them, in the slip's paper, with a silk tassel. Covers lift on hover.

Ebru endpapers

The inside of a kitab's covers: two leaves of marbled paper in lapis, oxblood and ochre, framed by the leather turn-ins and darkening into the fold. Paper labels in the folio's jadwal are pasted on: Fees and Family discounts on the left, Class Timings on the right. The marbling is SVG noise (like the wood grain), so it costs no images. In dark mode it's the same marbling in deeper colours, with dark labels.

The ijazah

One sheet, like the certificate a student works toward. An illuminated unwan heads it: a gilt band with a row of lapis finials, a lapis field of scrolling rumi vine, and a cartouche with the site's name, "Alimiyyah Program". It's floral, with no stars and no arch. Beneath, the terms in two columns under red rubrics, with dotted leaders, inside a jadwal of gold, ink and red. The masjid's logo is pressed in as a red ink seal at the foot, and turns gold in dark mode.

Card catalogue

A drawer of the library's catalogue: three typed index cards on staggered tabs (Fees, Family discounts, Class Timings), ruled in blue with the red header line. Click a tab to bring its card forward; the tabs are a keyboard tablist too. The drawer front is the shelf's wood with a brass label holder and a cup pull, and becomes glass in dark mode. It's the most "library" of the five and the least Islamic, and only one card shows at a time.

The prayer-times board

The board that hangs in every masjid: a wooden frame round a black grooved face, the masjid's name and a gold headline, and every time in slotted numerals, split across the middle like the tiles on a salah board. The timings are a grid, days down the side and the two tracks across (a dash where Part-time has no Saturday). Fees and Family discounts sit beneath like a board's Jumu'ah strip. On phones the grid becomes one list per track.

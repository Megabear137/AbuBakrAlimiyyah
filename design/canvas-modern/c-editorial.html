Introduction

A full course of study in Qur'an, hadith, fiqh, Arabic and the sciences that serve them, taught in the traditional way: text by text, with a teacher, in the company of fellow students.

Our Program

Alimiyyah

Our Alimiyya program is a seven year program, from which the first four years are part time and the last three years are full time. Throughout this course, students will attain a mastery of the Arabic language, through which they will gain a deep understanding of the Qur'an and Hadith.

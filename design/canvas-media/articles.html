Five more ways to set out Articles

None of these is a row of cards like Teachers. Each one keeps the Brothers / Sisters switch that is already on the site, and every switch works. Titles, dates and authors are sample placeholders, not real content. For comparison, the current version is option 3 on the first mockups page.

A · Front page B · Open kitab C · Reading timeline D · Bento & pull quote E · Folios on the desk
A

Front page

Set like the front page of a journal, with no cards at all. The newest article leads on the left under a double green-and-gold rule, with a gold drop capital. The rest are listed in a numbered index on the right. Typographic and calm. The latest article gets most of the attention.

Articles

B

Open kitab

A book lying open in its green binding, which ties it to the curriculum shelf. The left page holds the latest article under an illuminated heading band, with a justified opening paragraph. The right page is a table of contents with dotted leaders to the reading time. Page numbers are Arabic numerals. The most traditional option. On a phone the two pages stack.

Articles

C

Reading timeline

Full-width rows down a gold thread, newest first. Each row has a large date, a diamond on the line, the title and a short excerpt. Hovering a row lays a white panel behind it, turns the diamond and fills the arrow. Best when articles are posted regularly, because the dates show the rhythm.

    D

    Bento & pull quote

    Tiles of different sizes. A tall green feature with a faint star lattice sits beside a wide white tile, a mint tile quoting a line from one of the articles, and a small tile. The liveliest option. The pull quote draws readers in without a picture.

    E

    Folios on the desk

    Cream sheets laid slightly askew on a green desk. Each has a gold seal with its subject's initial, a folded corner and the author's name as an italic signature. Hovering a sheet straightens it and lifts it off the desk. The most tactile option: it feels like handwritten letters rather than web cards.
